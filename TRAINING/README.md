# Instructions

Make your own model!

The goal is to design, train, and test your own model that takes in X-Ray images and outputs the 10 measurements as accurately as possible. Feel free to reuse/copy paste code from data_utils in any of the existing folders (since it's not productive to reinvent the wheel for loading in data), but try to refrain from straight copy and paste for model.py, train.py, and test.py

The goal isn't necessarily performance, but more to experiment and familiarize yourself with pytorch. Your model can be as simple as a couple convolutional and FC layers.

Make a directory under your name to begin working.
